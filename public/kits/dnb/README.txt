Drum & bass kit — free drum sample kit from fingerdrum.io by Re: TUNES
https://fingerdrum.io/sample-kits#dnb

A studio kit pitched up like a sped-up break: a cracking snare, a short punchy kick, tight hats and a washy ride.

Inside: one folder per sound, 16-bit 48 kHz stereo WAV.
File names: <sound>[_soft|_medium|_hard]_<variant>. Soft and hard are velocity layers; variants are round robins
(the same hit played again), so alternate them to avoid the machine-gun effect.
